My Background

I was born on May 11, 2006, in Houston, Texas, and moved to Fayetteville, Arkansas, when I was 8 years old.
It was there that I got my diploma from Fayetteville High School and fell in love with the world of library science after volunteering at our school library.
I always had a love of reading and storytelling, often staying up late into the night just to finish a series, but volunteering made me understand that I could help others to foster this passion too.
With the help of the librarians, my parents, and all of my wonderful friends, I’ve been working to make that dream come true.
